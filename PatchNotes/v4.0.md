## Version: 4.0 - 1.21.1 Update

### World Compatibility Notice
Existing worlds are **incompatible** with this version. This change is necessary due to our transition from **Forge to NeoForge**. 

While we aim for a seamless experience, a few **key mods have not yet been ported** to the new platform, which may result in missing features or broken functionality in legacy saves. To ensure the best experience and full stability, a **new world is required**.

For bug reports and issues, please visit our [Issue Tracker](https://github.com/AMPZNetwork/Kingdoms-of-the-Valley/issues)
